## 🔰 About the Project

**Egypt Future 2030** is an interactive website showcasing Egypt's vision for digital transformation and sustainable development. The project presents:

- **Vision 2030** strategic goals
- **National projects** (43 projects) including the New Administrative Capital, Benban Solar Power Station
- **Artificial Intelligence** initiatives and National Strategy (2021)
- **Smart cities** development (31 cities)
- **Interactive statistics** and dynamic charts (Chart.js)
- **Smart chatbot** with AI-powered responses and local storage
- **Story Timeline** (2014-2030) showing Egypt's transformation journey
- **FAQ Page (QA.html)** with search and category filtering
- **Projects Page (projects.html)** with flip cards showcasing national projects
- **Before/After Comparisons** for major national projects
- **Lotus Tech Loading Screen** with premium animations and neon effects

## ✨ Features

### 🎨 Visual Features
- **Lotus Tech Loading Screen**: Premium animated preloader with neon cyan effects, particles, and 3D grid background.
- **Video Background**: Hero section with "Planet Digital Earth" autoplay video background.
- **Glass Morphism**: Modern glass effect design applied throughout the interface.
- **GSAP Animations**: Advanced entrance and scroll-triggered animations using GSAP and ScrollTrigger.
- **Neon Effects**: Electric cyan (#00eaff) and blue neon glow effects for a futuristic look.
- **Lottie Animations**: High-performance vector-based animations.

### 📱 Interactive Elements
- **Smart Chatbot**: AI-powered assistant for Egypt 2030 queries with persistent chat history.
- **Interactive Charts**: Dynamic visualizations using Chart.js (Pie, Line, Bar, and Doughnut charts).
- **Project Showcase**: Interactive carousel and flip cards with detailed popup modals.
- **Story Timeline**: Interactive journey (2014-2030) with dot navigation and scroll-based progress.
- **Before/After Sliders**: Interactive image comparisons for Administrative Capital, Benban, and New Alamein.
- **FAQ System**: Fully searchable FAQ with accordion-style answers and category filtering.

### 🖥️ Technical Features
- **Responsive Design**: Optimized for all screen sizes (Mobile, Tablet, Desktop).
- **RTL Support**: Full Arabic language support with appropriate typography.
- **SEO Optimized**: Complete meta tags, Open Graph, and structured data for better search visibility.
- **Performance**: Optimized assets, lazy loading, and local library hosting for speed.
- **Presentation Deck**: Complete 10-slide interactive presentation included in the repository.

### 📊 Content Features
- **31 Smart Cities**: Comprehensive details on Egypt's 4th generation cities.
- **43 National Projects**: In-depth look at major infrastructure and technology projects.
- **210+ Digital Services**: Statistics and info on Egypt's digital government services.
- **AI Strategy**: Details on the National AI Strategy launched in 2021.
- **Team Profiles**: Professional profiles of the Freedom522 development team.
